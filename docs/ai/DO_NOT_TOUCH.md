# Do Not Touch

Unless explicitly approved by the user, the following actions are forbidden:
- Do not push to GitHub (`git push`).
- Do not deploy to Vercel.
- Do not apply Supabase production migrations.
- Do not upload Android builds to Google Play (AAB/APK).
- Do not modify Google Play closed testing settings.
- Do not change production environment variables/secrets.
- Do not expose `service_role` keys.
- Do not modify billing or payment webhook handlers.
- Do not modify Facebook App SDK integration.
- Do not add paid infrastructure.
- Do not implement real WhatsApp APIs.
- Do not expand complex CRM features.
- Do not reset, clean, or stash changes without approval.
